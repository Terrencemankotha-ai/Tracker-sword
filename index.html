            About

            Abraham Sword Tracker · version 2.0
            Official inflation for USD, EUR, JPY, GBP and AUD, with live markets, news and your own watchlist.

            Sources and notes
            • United States: BLS CPI-U, not seasonally adjusted. The Fed's 2% target is defined on PCE.
            • Euro area: HICP via the ECB Data Portal (Eurostat data). The newest month is often a flash estimate.
            • Japan: CPI via OECD (follows the Statistics Bureau of Japan release by a few days). Core excludes fresh food.
            • United Kingdom: ONS CPI annual rate (D7G7); core is DKO8.
            • Australia: ABS monthly CPI; core is the trimmed mean.
            • Policy rates: Federal Reserve (middle of the target range), ECB deposit rate, Bank of Japan, Bank of England, RBA. Kept in data/policy_rates.json.
            • Live prices, market overview, heat map, live wire and economic calendar: TradingView. Headlines: each outlet's public news feed, checked every 30 minutes.
