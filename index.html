    Sources and notes
    • United States: BLS CPI-U, not seasonally adjusted. The Fed's 2% target is defined on PCE.
    • Euro area: HICP via the ECB Data Portal (Eurostat data). The newest month is often a flash estimate.
    • Japan: CPI via OECD (follows the Statistics Bureau of Japan release by a few days). Core excludes fresh food.
    • United Kingdom: ONS CPI annual rate (D7G7); core is DKO8.
    • Australia: ABS monthly CPI; core is the trimmed mean.
